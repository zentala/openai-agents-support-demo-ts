title: Refunds
---
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we
receive the returned item. The refund can never be higher than the price paid
for the item. Refunds above 500 PLN are checked by a human colleague before they
are issued, which can take up to one business day. Delivery fees are refunded
only when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
