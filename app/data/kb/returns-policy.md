title: Returns policy
---
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, in the original packaging
and unused. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your order, not the
order date. Custom-made items and mattresses that were unpacked cannot be
returned. To start a return, tell us the order number and the item. Return
shipping for items over 30 kg is collected from your home free of charge.
After the 30 days, returns are not possible, but the warranty still applies.
