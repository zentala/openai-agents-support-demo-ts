Three kinds of message, three looks:
1. AI assistant — plain text with an "AI assistant" label above every model answer.
2. Human — green bubble with the person's name.
3. Automatic step (rule in code) — not a message at all: a centered two-line card that reports what the system did, a segmented ticket badge (Ticket | ID | status) and a grey "Automatic step" badge. No speaker, no bubble.
Sources the AI used are clickable chips with the article title; a click opens the article.
Short status changes (someone joined or left) are thin centered lines.
